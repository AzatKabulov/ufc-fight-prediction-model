# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FightIQ is used by someone reviewing UFC cards, individual matchups, model outputs, and prediction history. The primary user is a technical MMA analyst or builder who wants fast fight-level insight, not a marketing walkthrough. Portfolio reviewers may also inspect it to understand the modeling workflow and product thinking.

## Product Purpose

FightIQ helps users inspect upcoming UFC fights, generate predictions, review model and market signals, add fight-week intelligence, and track prediction accuracy over time. Success means the interface feels credible, fast, and transparent: users can see what the model predicts, why it predicts it, and how previous predictions performed.

## Brand Personality

Technical, sharp, focused.

The current frontend direction should be preserved: dark fight-night atmosphere, compact product surfaces, red accent color, strong matchup typography, and dense but scannable analytics. Improvements should polish hierarchy, responsiveness, accessibility, and interaction states without redesigning the app.

## Anti-references

Do not make FightIQ look like a sportsbook, crypto dashboard, generic AI SaaS landing page, or flashy sports-news homepage. Avoid decorative gradients, over-marketed hero copy, oversized landing-page sections, and unnecessary visual noise. Keep the app grounded in analysis and prediction workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the current FightIQ identity before adding new styling.
2. Make prediction confidence and data quality easy to scan at a glance.
3. Keep dense information organized, not simplified into vague cards.
4. Separate model output, market signal, and fight-week intel clearly.
5. Favor familiar product UI patterns over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve visible focus states, maintain readable contrast, avoid color-only status communication, support reduced motion, and keep touch targets usable on mobile.
